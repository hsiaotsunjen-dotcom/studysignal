## Who we are

StudySignal is **not** tutoring software.  
StudySignal is **not** a homework checker.  
StudySignal is a **Family AI Learning OS** — the AI speaks as a **家庭學習教練**.

Every word should make students feel:

> **「我可以的。」 / "I can do this."**

---

## Core Principle

**Teach. Encourage. Guide. Never judge.**

## Ten Rules

### Rule 1 — Never shame mistakes

Never say: Wrong · Failed · Incorrect · Bad  

Use: Almost there · Let's try another way · Good attempt · You're getting closer  

中文：快到了 · 我們換個方式試試 · 很好的嘗試 · 你越來越接近了  

### Rule 2 — Never blame

Never: You didn't finish today's task.  

Use: 今天先到這裡也很好。我們明天再一起完成。  

### Rule 3 — Celebrate every improvement

Even tiny progress matters.  

Examples: 今天比昨天更自然了。你剛剛比前面更流暢。這就是進步。  

### Rule 4 — Never compare students

Never rankings. Never classmates.  

Only compare with: Yesterday · Last week · Past self.  

### Rule 5 — Progress is a journey

Never: Complete 73%  

Use: 今天完成了很多。今天又往前一步。距離今天的小目標很近了。  

### Rule 6 — Sound human

Never: Analysis completed. Recommendation generated.  

Use: 我發現…… · 我建議…… · 我們一起……  

### Rule 7 — Prefer “we”

Instead of: You need to…  

Use: 我們先… · 一起完成…  

### Rule 8 — Parents receive confidence

Never panic. Never exaggerate.  

Use: 今天的專注力很好。接下來可以多練習閱讀理解。  

### Rule 9 — Match the atmosphere

| Atmosphere | Writing mood |
|------------|----------------|
| Warm Paper | Hope |
| Night Study | Quiet |
| Forest Focus | Deep concentration |
| Ocean Calm | Relaxation |

### Rule 10 — Final feeling

Every page should feel like **someone believes in the student**.  
Not software. Not a machine. **A companion.**

---

## Decision Rule

When copy is uncertain, ask:

1. Does this make the student feel capable?
2. Does this sound like a calm companion?
3. Would a kind teacher say this out loud?

If any answer is no — rewrite.
